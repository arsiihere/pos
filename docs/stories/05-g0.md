Words: **bill** = what one seating (dine-in) or one takeaway / parking order owes, built live from 02b's ledger until it closes; **split** = one part of a bill paid on its own (by items or by amount) with its own receipt; **payment** = one amount in one **payment mode** (an owner-configured list row: cash, card, online, …, R7); **close** = the last split is paid → the bill is final; **invoice** = the immutable money record of a closed split (number, lines, tax by class, rounding, payments); **refund** = money given back after close; **shift** = one person's period of taking payments (float, payments, petty cash, blind count, variance, D21); **drawer** = a cash drawer kicked by a receipt printer; **X report** = the running totals of an open day; **Z report** = the final totals of one business day (open → close, 01a S33).

## 4. Refined stories (pilot, milestone A 2027-02-28)

### Story 1 — See and print the bill (E3 counter receipt AC1–2; D11; 02a To 05)

*As a cashier (or an order taker with `take-payment` on own tables), I open a table's or a token's bill and print it for the customer.*

1. Opened from the cashier screen (bill-requested first, Q13), the table view or a token search. Dine-in = the seating's current orders (`seating_orders` at read time, merges and splits folded); takeaway / parking = the order; an order on an archived table stays billable (01a §6.5).
2. Lines from 02b `orderLines` only: name, options, units, `billed_minor`; COMP lines at their value with a COMP deduction (C4); **PENDING VOID** lines tagged, the total excludes their `refund_minor` only (04); a pending weight shown "Weight needed" with 0; return lines never shown as billed (03a §3.8a); removed / voided units gone.
3. Totals: subtotal → discount (story 4) → service charge (Q5) → tax by class (story 5) → rounding (config) → **total**; paid so far and balance when splits exist.
4. **Print bill** (pre-bill) through 03c on the counter printer; printing never changes money. A later round or a return clears `bill_requested_at` (02a) and the next print shows the new total.
5. The bill view refreshes on `event` frames of its orders and on focus; correctness never depends on the screen (close re-checks everything server-side).

### Story 2 — Take payment and close (E0 payments AC1, 2, 6; E8 split AC4–5; E3 receipt AC1, 3; D8, D39, R7)

*As a cashier with an open shift, I take one or more payments and close the bill; the receipt prints and the drawer opens for cash.*

1. Modes = the owner's list (cash, card, online, other; R7, K-7); card and online amounts typed in, optional or required reference per mode (config); cash may exceed the balance → **change due**; non-cash never exceeds the balance.
2. Payments can be added and removed before the close; each is logged separately (E8 AC4) against the **taker's** open shift (Q9); no open shift → `409 SHIFT_NOT_OPEN`.
3. **Close is refused** while: any pending void on the bill's orders (04 `pendingVoids`, D11), any pending weight with `live_units > 0` (01c V18, 04 R-4), a pending discount request, a balance ≠ 0, a split not paid (K-3). All checked in the close transaction (F §1 race table "void approved while cashier closes").
4. Close (online only, never device-queued, G0-1) writes in one transaction: `InvoiceIssued` per split (number, lines, taxes, rounding, payments) → at the last split `OrdersBilled { order_ids, bill_id }` and, for dine-in, `BillClosed { seating_id }` (K-1) → closes the bill's open `comp` requests `obsolete` (02b) → receipt `printDocument` with `{ drawer }` when cash was taken (03c §3.9).
5. `BillClosed` reaches staff sockets and cashier kiosks (02a-N3): the tile turns NEEDS_CLEANING live.
6. Printer offline or none → close still succeeds; the receipt waits in 03c's queue; the screen shows the receipt (E8 edge "share via QR" is off in P1, D20).
7. Lock never matters (D39): `take-payment` with its scope is the only check.

### Story 3 — Split a bill (E8 split AC1–3, 5; Q3)

1. By items (choose lines / units per split) or by amount (amounts that sum to the total; integer paisa, the remainder explicitly assigned, E8 AC3); "split equally into N" is a shortcut of by-amount.
2. Each split has its own discount share, tax, rounding, payments, number and receipt; the bill closes when every split is paid (K-3).
3. A void or comp on a line that sits in an unpaid split re-totals that split only; a paid split is final (changes after it are refunds, story 6).

### Story 4 — Discount and service charge (E0 payments AC4–5; N7, G1-23)

1. Discount on the bill or a split: fixed amount or percent; reason code (+ note for Other); `apply-discount` → `allowed` applies at once, `requires_approval` raises 01d kind `discount` (`details` amount + reason, `APPROVAL_TIER_STEPS`, PIN, approver from `answerer_staff_id`, `alert_id` on the effect event).
2. A discount never exceeds the pre-tax amount; it lowers the taxable base pro rata across the bill's lines (G0-5).
3. Service charge (Q5): a branch setting per order type, added automatically; removal with `remove-service-charge` + reason (approvable).
4. Every discount / removal: requester, approver, method, amount, reason, time (G0 N7) in the event and the audit view (`registerAuditAction`).

### Story 5 — Tax and rounding (E8 tax-invoice AC1–3; 01a S31, S34; 01c V11; D47)

1. A line is taxed only when the branch has `tax_enabled` and the line has a `tax_class_id` (K-4); the rate is read from `branchConfig()` at close (archived class still resolvable, S22); per bill type and payment method as data (01a v0.7; mixed methods → Q4).
2. Deal: the deal's class on the deal line's billed amount; option child: its own class; never on `allocated_price_minor` (01c).
3. Tax inclusive or exclusive of menu prices, the rounding method and step (e.g. nearest Rs 5 / 10, Q30 of the field list) are **branch settings** (config); all arithmetic in integers, rounded once per tax class per split, then the split total rounded once (D47); the rounding difference prints as its own line.
4. Bill / receipt show NTN (template), tax per class, total tax, total incl. tax (E8 AC1).

### Story 6 — Refund after close (E0 refunds AC1–2, 5; Q8)

1. On a closed split: refund by lines / units or by amount, reason required, `apply-refund` (approvable); paid out from the refunder's open shift in a chosen mode (cash out of the drawer, or card / online reversal recorded).
2. Refunds never touch 02b's ledger or 04's voids (`ORDER_BILLED` stays); the invoice stays immutable; the Z report lists refunds by mode; the food's fate (wastage) is recorded on the refund.
3. Σ refunds on a split ≤ what it collected (invariant, property test).

### Story 7 — Shift: open, float, blind count, close (D21; F §4.6; E0 shifts AC1, 3–5; E8 Z AC1–3; Q9, Q10)

1. A person with `manage-own-shift` opens a shift with an opening float (0 allowed for a no-cash shift); a drawer (kiosk + its receipt printer's drawer) belongs to at most one open cash shift.
2. Close: **blind count** — counted cash entered before expected is shown; then expected = float + cash taken − change + petty in − petty out − cash refunds; variance shown.
3. Variance above the branch threshold (config) → 01d request `shift_variance` to a manager (R22); the shift closes on approve.
4. Skipping the count only with `skip-blind-count` (default Owner), always logged (D21 AC5).
5. Card / online totals per mode are shown at close for checking against the terminal / account (no automatic reconciliation, P2).
6. Takeover on a kiosk with an open shift → Q10.

### Story 8 — Petty cash (E0 shifts AC2; Q15)

1. Cash in / cash out against the taker's open cash shift, amount + reason code (+ note), `record-petty-cash`; above the branch threshold → approval (01d).
2. Counted in the shift's expected cash and on the Z report.

### Story 9 — Day close and Z report (E0 shifts AC6–7; E8 Z AC4–5; D21; Q11)

1. Day close refused (`409 DAY_CLOSE_BLOCKED`, 04's code and list shape, new `reason`s) while any shift is open, any void request is open (04, kept), and per Q11 for unbilled orders.
2. Writes `ZReportIssued` (K-5) after `stockEffects` and before `BusinessDayClosed`, in one transaction; prints it (03c) and shows it on the hub; PDF / CSV when the printer is offline is the browser's print / CSV of the same query.
3. Z content: sales (gross, discounts, service charge, net, tax by class, rounding), payments by mode, refunds by mode, comps (value), voided comps (`comp_voided_minor`, O21), voids by disposition (wastage / staff meal / moved / other, value and count, 04 G-8), petty cash, per shift float / expected / counted / variance and total variance, bills count, average bill, open orders carried (Q11).
4. Open comp / discount requests at close: listed with `alerts.listOpen({ kind })` and closed `obsolete` with a note in the same transaction (01d To 05) — void requests block instead (04 A-1).
5. **X report** = the same query on the open day, any time, `view-reports`.

### Story 10 — Billing settings (R7, R12; E0 template AC1–4; K-12)

1. In the owner app (01a branch settings, cloud → hub snapshot): payment modes (name, kind cash / card / online / other, reference required, active, order), rounding method and step, prices include tax yes / no, service charge % per order type, variance threshold, petty-cash approval threshold, receipt template parts (K-12), receipt / Z printer choice from 03c's list (hub admin, where printers live).
2. Template changes apply to the next print; queued jobs are unchanged (E0 AC4).
