## 4. Refined stories (pilot, milestone A 2027-02-28)

### Story 1 — Void a line past stage 1 (E6 item lifecycle; D10, D29; E0 refunds AC4–5)

*As a staff member with `void-item`, I void units of a line that is cooking, cooked or served, so the customer is not charged; the food goes to ready stock or wastage.*

1. Allowed on a line of an **unbilled** order whose units are past stage 1 (engine stage `cooking`, `done`, `served`, or the order left the engine). Stage 1 → `409 ITEM_WRONG_STAGE` with the hint to Remove (02b). Billed → `409 ORDER_BILLED` (refund is 05's).
2. Partial by `units` (01c §3.4.8): per unit on `piece`, `deal`, `deal_part`, `option_child`; g / ml lines whole; a partial void of a deal with an open g/ml part refused `gml_deal_part_partial` (02b §3.4).
3. Money from 02b's ledger only (`planVoid`): uncomped units first; a comped unit moves to `comp_voided_minor` (O21); cascades to option children and deal parts with caps; one `ItemVoided` per touched line.
4. **Disposition** (owner 2026-11-07): stage 2–3 shows **Move to stock** (pre-selected when the voider holds `move-item-to-stock`, else disabled) and **Wastage**; an item with no stock draw shows Move to stock disabled, "Stock not tracked for this item". Served or out-of-engine → Wastage only. `moved` writes `ItemMovedToStock` then `ItemVoided` (K-3) with `stockEffects`; the next order takes the portion from stock (02b §3.2 step 8).
5. **Reason** required (Q4). Same transaction: `ItemVoided` (+ `ItemMovedToStock`), `stockEffects`, `lineChangeDrafts` (order cancelled when emptied, food cleared, stale comp requests closed). Zone counts never change.
6. Grant `allowed` → applied at once. Grant `requires_approval` → story 2. Online only (PIN-capable, not queued; 02b O13 precedent).
7. A remade dish (`ItemReturned remake`) stays billable and is voidable like any served line (O20). Customer's own wrong order → Q12.

### Story 2 — Pending void and approval (D11; E-REQ R22; F §4.5)

1. A void needing approval raises `void_approval` (01d): `APPROVAL_TIER_STEPS`, `needs_pin: 'always'`, `right { approval, base: 'void-item', range: 'approve-void' }`; `details` = item(s), units, amount, reason, disposition, stage; the approver approves or rejects on their own device or in person.
2. While **pending**: the units stay on the bill as **PENDING VOID** and are excluded from the total (05 reads `pendingVoids`); bill close refused (05); merge / split refused (`registerMergeGuard`); behaviour of other actions on those lines per Q7.
3. **Approve** → story 1's effect, recomputed now (`apply`, read-only, approver = `answerer_staff_id`, `alert_id` on the events). Order billed or lines changed meanwhile → refused in `preRead` before any PIN (01d §3.3 rule 4 (b)).
4. **Reject** → nothing changes on the bill; requester gets `request_result` (01d); the table's holder gets the rejected-void notice (Q11).
5. **Withdraw** (requester only) → as if it never happened (Q10).
6. **Override** (OVERRIDDEN) → Q9.

### Story 3 — Immutable audit log view (E6 audit log AC1–5; F §3.1)

1. Append-only by construction: the log is built from the event store (immutable, D52); no command edits or deletes it.
2. Each row: time (hub clock), actor id + **name at the time**, action, target (table / order / line / staff / device), reason, outcome, device; approvals show approver, **method** (`pin` / `in_person`) and "approved on the requester's device" (01d R2).
3. Filters: date range, staff member, action type; newest first, paged. Export to CSV (Q13).
4. Visible with `view-audit-log` (Q15). Actions covered: Q14.
