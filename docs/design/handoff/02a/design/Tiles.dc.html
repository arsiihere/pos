Six states — icon + word + colour; free is quiet, urgent is loud

Lock, holder, assignee, joined, call sent, waiting to send

Flags, queued clean, archived, offline (dim), pressed

Puck — state icon; its ring fills over 60 min open. Solid puck = needs action (Bill, late, void).
Avatars — solid = holder (maroon = you). Hollow first = the zone’s assignee when the holder is someone else.
Footer — padlock + who is ordering; one flag (late, void, call, waiting to send). Free tiles show seats.
