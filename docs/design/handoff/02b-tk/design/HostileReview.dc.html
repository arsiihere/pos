Hostile review — 1:30 PM, 15 people in the queue

1 · Unpaid bag handed over

Mixed pay-first / pay-later means some ready bags are unpaid.

Before (v1 of this design)

Same 1-tap “Hand over” on an unpaid tile: the bag leaves, the bill stays open, nobody notices until day close.

After (frames in this hand-off)

An unpaid ready tile has only Take payment. Hand over appears after Paid. Board shows “Pay at counter”. Token view keeps “Hand over” off with “Pay first”.

2 · Wrong send button

Under pressure the cashier hits whatever is saffron.

Before (v1 of this design)

Two saffron buttons side by side → wrong one at speed; unpaid orders walk out.

After (frames in this hand-off)

One primary (Send & pay, wider, 72 px). Pay later is plain and narrower. If it is pressed, the token row and tile carry a red Due until paid.

3 · Packer hands over unpaid

Sometimes the kitchen pass hands bags over.

Before (v1 of this design)

54Waiting at counter

Packer hands bag 54 to the customer from the pass — it was never paid.

After (frames in this hand-off)

54Waiting at counter
Due Rs 1,800 — customer pays at counter

Due shows on the pass card; Handed over is off until paid (hub check in GAPS G-7).

4 · Offline cash lost

Hub drops for 2 minutes in the rush.

Before (v1 of this design)

Offline, Send & pay sends, then the pay step fails; the cashier already took Rs 5,000 cash and the payment is never recorded.

After (frames in this hand-off)

Payment needs the hub. Send now; take payment when Live.

Offline the primary becomes Send (queues). The order is Due, so it shows red when the hub is back and cannot be handed over unpaid.

6 · Quantity slip / lost cart

Fat-finger at the edge; tab tapped by habit.

Before (v1 of this design)

×8 and ×10 at the screen edge: a slip gives 10 naan.

Tap “Parking” with 11 items in the cart → cart gone (today’s bug, kept in v1 because the draft was “saved”).

After (frames in this hand-off)

Four chips, and the line count updates in 20 px next to the stepper; more than 8 is typed (“10n”).

Leave guard on every exit (tab, Back, sign out, deep link): Hold / Throw away / Stay. Sign out and idle lock hold automatically. Held list is per counter, not per person (GAPS G-6).
