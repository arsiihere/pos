## 2. Attack results by area

1. **Races in one tick.** The serial writer makes Open vs Open, Move vs Open, Merge vs Touch, and Take-over vs Accept safe as transactions. The bugs are what each one computes: the self-handover deadlock (6), the take-over that 403s on the withdraw (4), the queued Clean on a new party (15), and the submit onto NEEDS_CLEANING (11).
2. **Replay and reset identity.** The folds are event-pure except the lock-activity refresh (3). `ItemServed` by `stream_id` is right. `OrderLinesAdded` is missing from the fold list (12). `TableCleaned` / `TablesMerged` deleting the lock row from their fold is replay-safe.
3. **Idempotency (D32).** `processed_commands` stores 200 responses only (`commands/route.ts:147-170`), so the no-op replies (`opened: false`, `requested: false`, `repeated: true`) replay correctly. Errors re-execute, which is acceptable. The real idempotency hole is cross-session: queued commands keyed by `table_id` (15).
4. **Statement counts / N+1.** `table_team` (1), `tableOwners` (1), `zoneOf` (0), `table-map` (3 + 1), and tick idle (1) are fine. The handover details read is unstated (16). The cited session index is wrong (18).
5. **01d / 00 / 03a interfaces.** `defineAlertKind` fields, `alerts.raise` input, `registerTick` and `registerTargetResolver` match the code. Mismatches: withdraw (4), stale accept (5), `targetKey` and owner semantics (1), `seen` on a team kind (7), the card details shape (16), and the 03a `tableOwners` value type (8).
6. **Owner rules (Q1 / Q2 / B / R23).** Take-over without acceptance: correct. Handover needs acceptance: correct, except the self case (6). The holder dropped from waiter alerts contradicts Q2 (2). Merge and cross-zone move bypass R23 (14).
7. **Permission and scope (C8).** Scope comes last everywhere except Take-over (26). Handler-side second permissions need the `requires_approval` rule (26).
8. **Error codes vs F §6.2.** There is one duplicate (23) and three class choices (24). Every status in §12.2 exists in §9 or the catalogue.
9. **Carried rows.** The §4.6 split of the "To 02" rows is complete. New rows are needed To 03a (8, 9), To 02b (11, 12, 15), To 05 (32) and To 01d (1, 7, maybe 16).
10. **Over-engineering.** `source` (17) and the waiter-only `direct` (2) add logic nobody asked for. Everything else traces to a T-decision or an owner answer.
