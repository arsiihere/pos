- Packed fired lines are packed against COOKING lines and each other, not against frozen lines' planned intervals (S:272 "hidden and frozen lines are placed against the packed result"). With a frozen line in the way, the live profile can exceed capacity until the next Start's cascade (D42), and the slip covers that. Test 115's "no instant has 2 units" holds for its fixture, which has no frozen line. Consistent with F §4.7 D33 / D42, so not a finding.
