## 2. Attack results by area

1. **Races inside the single writer.** Last stock unit, token, ids and comp approval are serial and safe as transactions. The bugs are in what is computed: the zone count across lines of one payload (6), the comp request left open after a remove (7), and the money ledger between comp, remove and void (1).
2. **Idempotency and queued replay.** D32 and M19 cover a lost answer. H-1 is incomplete (8) and opens an impersonation path (9); a double Send yields a false refusal (29).
3. **Merge / split / move.** `follow_up`, `seatingTotals` and scopes go through `seating_orders` and are right live; the replayed `BillClosed` fold is not (15); a split puts two parties in one slip group (16).
4. **Batch order vs 03a :615.** The order of `OrderPlaced`, `ItemAdded`, `StockReserved` is right; the zone `ItemAdded`s after it are writer bugs as built (2); `ZoneStockLow` is not an event (17).
5. **Stage rules.** Partial remove releases the whole reservation (4); Served before fire and the fired-not-started remove both diverge from F §4.2 without an F row (10).
6. **Comp money vs 01c per-unit allocation.** No shared unit ledger (1); child and deal units unstated (25).
7. **Zones.** The nesting the routing relies on does not exist (5); serving raita makes the next round a follow-up (3); waiters cannot refill (13); the mistaken raita has no cheap undo (26).
8. **Statement counts and N+1.** `ReleaseItems` (20); counts missing for most commands and the catch-up (21).
9. **Invented names or shapes.** `meta` envelope (8), `ZoneStockLow` (17), `planOrder` and `ProposedOrder` drift (18), `ORDER_NOT_FOUND` (22), the zone parent and floor route (5).
10. **Privacy.** Phone and name to every staff device and into the cloud log (11).
11. **§4.5 rows (R29 cost).** Q-3 (2), Q-4 (4), H-1 (8), B-3 (13), B-4 (14) are incomplete or uncosted; E-1, F-1 and 02a O9 lack rows (23).
