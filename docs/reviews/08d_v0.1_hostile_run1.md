## BLOCKER

### B-1 — `business_date` is not a key: two business days can share it
**Where:** S1 (l. 70, stream id `sheet:<business_date>`), S9 (l. 78), S13 (l. 82), S14 (l. 83), §5.2 `inventory_counts` PK (l. 199–205), `daily_sheets` PK (l. 214), §5.3 `CountRecorded` / `DailySheetClosed` (l. 243–257), §6.4 / §6.6 payloads (l. 302, 319), S12 `theoreticalUsage(db, { business_dates })` (l. 81), R-c (l. 141).
**Problem:** 01a S33 makes the business day an action, and `business_date` is only the hub's local date at the open ("`business_date` is only a label", 01a v2.8 S55 l. 323). 01a test 143 (l. 1586) is literally *open 06:00 → close 10:00 → open again* on the same date; `day_min_gap_hours` may be 0. A lunch-only close and an evening reopen, or a close at 00:30 and a reopen at 05:00 (paye breakfast), give **two days with one label**. 08d keys streams, counts, sheets, the "previous date" chain and the 08c call by that label, so: the second day's counts silently supersede the first day's; `daily_sheets` holds one row for two days; the second close is refused `409 INVENTORY_SHEET_CLOSED` (or must "supersede" a different day); the chain test "previous business date" is undefined.
**Fix:** key every 08d day reference by **`business_day_id` = the `event_id` of `BusinessDayOpened`** — the repo already does this (`PresenceSet.business_day_id`, `apps/hub/src/alerts/commands/set-presence.ts:21–38`). Keep `business_date` in every payload as the C1 label. Stream id `sheet:<business_day_id>`; PKs `(business_day_id, …)`; commands take `business_day_id` (default open day / last closed day); "previous" = the previous `BusinessDayOpened` by `seq`, not date − 1; R-c becomes `theoreticalUsage(db, { business_day_ids })`. Moves carry the open day's id too. Tests: two days with one date → two sheets.

### B-2 — The cooked-count contract with 08b does not exist, and is ambiguous where it does
**Where:** S10 (l. 79), S11 (l. 80), §4.1 row 08b (l. 122), R-b (l. 140), §5.3 `CountRecorded.cooked` (l. 246), §6.6 script "cooked equivalents (08b explosion)" (l. 320).
**Problem:** (1) 08d consumes "08b §4.2 `cookedFormsOf(itemId)`" — 08b v0.1 §4.2 (l. 122 of 08b) exposes no such function, and its `explodeUsage(inputs, shape, { stopAt })` takes a **sale-line shape** (`menu_item` / deal), not a `prepared` stock item and a quantity, so "raw-equivalent = qty × AP ÷ batch_yield_qty through 08b's explosion" has no callable. A forward reference outside an Exposes contract fails the checklist. (2) The cooked count is nested **under one A-item** (`CountRecorded { stock_item_id: <A-item>, cooked: [...] }`). PAYE-COOKED's batch recipe consumes **paye and ghee** — both on the A-item list (OQ-5). The same pot is then either entered twice (under paye and under ghee, two numbers that can disagree) or once and missing from the other A-item's closing — either way one A-item's actual usage is wrong. Cooked waste (S11) has the same shape problem. (3) EP lines and nested prepared items in the batch need yield / recursion rules that 08b's explosion owns, not 08d.
**Fix:** (a) Count and waste **the cooked item as its own entry**: `CountRecorded { stock_item_id: <prepared item>, kind, raw: null, cooked_qty }` (or a separate `entries` kind), allowed when the prepared item's published batch recipe contains ≥ 1 A-item. (b) At close, raw-equivalent per A-item = Σ over every counted cooked item of `explodeStock(inputs, preparedId, qty)[A-item]`. (c) Put a row on 08b (R-b): expose `explodeStockItem(inputs, stockItemId, qty: Fraction) → { usage: Map, unknown }` (AP via yield, recursion, depth 3, the same code `explodeUsage` uses) and derive "cooked forms of X" from `CostInputs` in 08d itself (no `cookedFormsOf` needed). Until 08b's text carries that row, 08d cannot be approved.

### B-3 — A sheet's period is not bounded by the count, so late sales fall between two sheets
**Where:** S13 (l. 82), S14 (l. 83 "a sheet may be closed for the **open** business day, or for the **last closed** day while no newer day is open"), §6.6 (l. 320), test 22 (l. 416).
**Problem:** (1) Closing the sheet while the day is open is allowed. Count at 22:00, close the sheet, sell four plates of paye until 23:30: those sales are in no sheet's theoretical (this sheet already closed, the next starts with the next day), but they *are* gone from tomorrow's physical stock, whose opening is tonight's 22:00 count → **tomorrow is flagged `over` for food that was sold and billed.** The same happens to receipts and waste recorded after the closing count (night delivery, a dropped tray after counting). (2) Conversely, once the next morning's `OpenBusinessDay` runs, last night's day can never be counted or closed: the manager who forgets to press Close at night loses the sheet for good and the next sheet is `chain_broken` — the paper fallback's "signed next morning" is impossible in the system. Opening the day is the first thing staff do (01a: Start day action on the stock page), so this will happen in week one.
**Fix:** `CloseDailySheet` only for a **closed** business day (after `BusinessDayClosed`); counts may be entered while the day is open. Allow count and close for **any closed day that has no sheet, provided no later day has a sheet** (closes stay in order; a newer open day does not block). Moves stay attributed to the day open when they were recorded. A move recorded for an item *after* its closing count on the same day (by `seq`) is shown on the Close screen as "after count — recount?" and the close needs a recount or an explicit confirm (frozen in the event as `moves_after_count`). Re-write test 22 and add: sheet for yesterday closed after today's open → accepted; sheet for D−2 when D−1 has a sheet → `409`.

## MAJOR

### M-1 — Cooked part: "not counted" silently becomes 0
**Where:** S9 (l. 78), §6.4 payload `cooked?: [...]` (l. 302), S14 close rule (l. 83, raw part only), §3.3 l. 109.
**Problem:** 08-g0 §4 "'Not counted' ≠ 0; all A-items must be entered to close". `cooked` is optional and defaults to `[]`; the close checks only the raw part. A store keeper who counts raw paye and forgets the degh closes with cooked = 0 → actual usage overstated by the leftover → a false `over` every night the degh is not entered. This is the exact failure the story names.
**Fix:** with B-2's shape, every counted cooked item (cooked form of an A-item) needs its own closing entry, `null` = not counted, `0` explicit; `409 INVENTORY_COUNT_MISSING` lists cooked items too. If the owner wants "no degh tonight" to be fast, the screen offers a "0" chip, never a blank default.

### M-2 — An explicit zero count cannot be entered
**Where:** §3.3 l. 103 (`qty_num` 1 … 10¹²), S9 (l. 78 "an explicit 0 is zero"), §6.4 edge "raw `0` → zero" (l. 307), test 12.
**Problem:** the only way to send a raw count is `{ unit_name, qty_num, qty_den }` and `qty_num ≥ 1`. "Raw 0" in §6.4 and test 12 contradicts the validation table; a sold-out ghee tin cannot be recorded.
**Fix:** counts: `qty_num` 0 … 10⁹ (receipts / waste keep ≥ 1); say it in §3.3 and test 12.

### M-3 — First sheet (and first sheet of a new A-item) has no defined opening
**Where:** S9 (l. 78 `opening_seed` "allowed only while the item has no closed sheet yet"), S13 `opening = previous closed sheet's closing, or opening_seed` (l. 82), S14 (l. 83), §6.6 (l. 320).
**Problem:** nothing requires an opening. The first `CloseDailySheet` (or the first after the owner ticks `is_a_item` on chanay in month two) with no previous sheet and no seed has `opening` undefined → `actual_usage` undefined; the spec neither refuses nor flags it.
**Fix (cut, preferred):** drop `opening_seed` entirely. An item with no previous closing is closed as **baseline**: its closing is recorded, `opening` / usage / variance null, `flag = 'unreliable'`, reason `no_opening`. That removes one `kind`, `INVENTORY_SEED_NOT_ALLOWED`, its rule and test, and costs one day of variance per new item. **Or** keep the seed and refuse the close (`409 INVENTORY_COUNT_MISSING { …, reason: 'opening' }`).

### M-4 — No way to correct a wrong receipt, transfer or waste
**Where:** S3–S8, S11 (l. 72–80), §6.1–§6.3; §12.4 says nothing.
**Problem:** every entry is append-only and only counts and sheets have a supersede. A typo of 18 500 kg for 18.5 kg (kg picked instead of g, or a double tap from two devices with two `command_id`s) cannot be undone: it stays in `received`, ruins that day's variance, and — worse — moves the MWA, whose value is then **frozen into every later `WasteRecorded.unit_cost_micro` and `DailySheetClosed.unit_cost_micro`** (S7 says itself that part is irreversible). §3.3's ceiling of 10¹² stock units does not stop it.
**Fix:** one more command + event, `ReverseInventoryEntry { event_id, note }` → `InventoryEntryReversed { reversed_event_id, note }` (permission of the original, needs a note, allowed while that day has no sheet). Projections treat the reversed event's lines as absent; `inventory_item_cost` for that item is **recomputed** from its priced lines (bounded: one item, ≤ a few hundred lines a year — state the statement count). Plus a plausibility guard: a line > 5 × the item's largest receipt so far needs `confirm_large: true` (screen asks "18 500 kg — sure?"). If the owner rejects the extra event, write the decision down in §12.4 with the cost ("a typo needs a written correction on the paper sheet; MWA stays wrong until the next priced receipt").

### M-5 — Flag rules miss the cases that matter
**Where:** S13 (l. 82), §5.3 l. 255–256, §6.6 edges (l. 329–330), test 5.
**Problems:** (1) `theoretical = 0` → `variance_bp = null` → the `pct` half of the AND can never be true → **5 kg of beef gone on a day with no beef sales is `ok`**. That is the purest theft signal there is. (2) The sign is never defined: which of `actual > theoretical` / `<` is `over`? (Only an edge row implies it.) (3) `unit_cost_micro` null (no MWA, no menu cost, I7 null) → `variance_value_minor` null → flag undefined. (4) Precedence: "`over` / `under` iff …; `unreliable` iff …; else `ok`" — when both hold, which wins? Before 08c *every* item is `unreliable`; once 08c is live, any `is_estimated` or coverage < 95 % hides a real `over`.
**Fix:** `over` ⇔ `variance_qty > 0`, `under` ⇔ `< 0`. Theoretical 0 and `variance_qty ≠ 0`: the pct test counts as passed (|bp| = ∞). Unit cost null → `unreliable` with reason `no_cost`, value null. Store **two fields**: `variance_flag ∈ ok | over | under | null` (from the thresholds alone) and `unreliable_reasons[]`; `flag` for the list view = `unreliable` if any reason, else `variance_flag`; the detail always shows `over`/`under` beside the reasons. Test 5 gains these four cases.

### M-8 — Who computes theoretical usage, and with which rules, is contradictory
**Where:** S12 (l. 81), R-c (l. 141), §4.1 row 08c (l. 123); 08b v0.1 §4.3 row 08d (08b l. 132), B11 (08b l. 76).
**Problem:** 08b says **08d** computes it ("Σ `explodeUsage` over the day's sale + comp lines, `stopAt` the counted cooked items"); 08d says **08c** exposes it. 08b's `stopAt` model (stop at PAYE-COOKED) is also incompatible with 08d's (explode to raw, convert cooked leftover to raw) — under `stopAt` the raw paye line's theoretical would be "batches cooked", which the pilot never records. And R-c leaves out rules that decide the variance: 04 V3 dispositions are `moved | wastage | staff_meal | other` (04 l. 67) — `staff_meal` is food made and eaten (D12: staff meals are usage), `moved` food is re-sold and must not count twice, `other` needs a policy; orders carried over (05 `OrdersCarriedOver`) and offline-queued orders replayed after day close need a day attribution; "coverage by revenue" has no owner (08b l. 133 gives it to "08c / 08d").
**Fix:** one ruling, written in 08d and 08b: 08c owns `theoreticalUsage` (it already reads sales and voids) **with full explosion, no `stopAt`**; 08b's 08d row is rewritten. R-c lists: sold + comp lines; voided lines excluded except `wastage` and `staff_meal` (→ `void_waste`, or a separate `staff_meal` figure); `moved` excluded; `other` → as `wastage` (flag `unreliable` reason `void_other` if the owner prefers); attribution = the `business_day_id` the line was placed in (B-1), carried-over orders stay with the day they were placed; coverage by revenue = 08c's, returned in the same call.

### M-9 — MWA `on_hand` (S7 a) is anchored by `seq`, not by day
**Where:** S7 (l. 76), §5.2 `inventory_moves_item_seq`, `inventory_counts_item_seq`, `inventory_item_cost.anchor_seq` (l. 197, 206, 212), §6.4 script "a closing raw count moves the anchor (1 update)" (l. 303), test 2.
**Problems:** (1) A count entered late (B-3's morning-after close, or a recount) has a `seq` after the new day's deliveries → "receipts since the anchor" drops those deliveries → `on_hand` too low → the next priced receipt over-weights its own price. (2) A `null` (not counted) closing count "moves the anchor" by the wording of §6.4. (3) "1 update" is only right if `on_hand := count` exactly, which is only true when no move follows the count by `seq` — the late case again needs a sum.
**Fix:** anchor = the latest **non-null raw closing count by business day** (B-1 order); `on_hand = anchor + Σ moves of later business days + moves of the anchor day recorded after the count (B-3)`. State the statements (1 read of the item's moves after the anchor day + 1 upsert). Test 2 gains: late count, recount, null count.

### M-10 — The engine hint beside the cooked count defeats the count
**Where:** S10 "The screen shows the engine's current level beside the cooked field as a hint ('system: 14')" (l. 79), §4.1 row 01c (l. 126), §6.5 `engine_level?` (l. 311), §8 item 1 (l. 358), test 14.
**Problem:** showing the expected number next to the field invites typing it (08-g0's own POST "blind mode" exists for this reason). The daily sheet exists to catch loss; a count that copies the system figure catches nothing, and the engine figure is reservations-adjusted, not physical, so it is also the wrong anchor. It is also the only reason 08d reads engine state.
**Fix (cut):** remove the hint, the `01c stockOf` consume row, `engine_level` and the test-14 part. If the owner wants a comparison, show it **after** the count is saved, on the Close screen.

### M-11 — Required per-command tests (Foundation §6.7, D32) are missing
**Where:** §10.2 (l. 392–410), §12.2 (l. 483–494).
**Problem:** Foundation §6.7 l. 1665: every hub command needs happy path, **one test per error code** and a **retransmit** test. Retransmit exists only for `ReceiveGoods` (test 9). `ReceiveTransfer` (test 10), `RecordWaste` (11), `RecordCount` (12, 13) and `CloseDailySheet` (15, 16) lack retransmit and most of their error codes (`404`, `422 not_convertible`, `409 BUSINESS_DAY_NOT_OPEN`, `INVENTORY_SEED_NOT_ALLOWED` …). §6.8 answers `404` without naming a code (§6.2 needs `<ENTITY>_NOT_FOUND`). **Fix:** one test row per command: "every §3.3 error it can return + retransmit"; §9 adds `INVENTORY_SHEET_NOT_FOUND` (404, `{ business_day_id }`) or §6.8 returns `200 { sheet: null }`.

### M-12 — Frozen costs lose their source; event shapes need it now (R29)
**Where:** S6 (l. 75 cost = MWA, else menu cost), S18 (l. 87), §5.3 `WasteRecorded.unit_cost_micro`, `DailySheetClosed.items[].unit_cost_micro` (l. 241, 255), 08a I12.

**Fix:** every frozen cost carries `unit_cost_source: 'mwa' | 'menu' | null` (S8 transfers: always `menu`). Report column label follows it. Fixture per event (m-11).
