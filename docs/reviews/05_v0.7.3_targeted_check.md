## Targeted checks (what holds)

- **Rounding property (v0.7.3 as written, non-`pro_rata`, 30 000 bills):**
  - Paying the shown due in its mode was never refused, and nothing was ever overpaid.
  - Every split that keeps paying its shown dues reaches balance 0.
  - Σ tender − change = total.
  - Card amounts are recorded exactly as keyed.
  - An all-card split has `R = 0`.
  - `R` ≠ 0 only when the cash net equals `roundToStep(G − D)`, so the rounding sits on the cash part.
  - The cash net is always a step multiple, `|R|` < step, and the shown cash due is always a step multiple.
  - Step 1 (`none`) never rounds.
  - Under `all` (Pakistan), `R = roundToStep(G) − G` whatever the payments, and both dues equal the balance.
  - The only failures are H11-1 (close and display after cash finishes) and, under `pro_rata`, H11-2.
- **Run-10 named cases:** G 12.02, card 10.00 + cash 2.00 → 12.00. G 12.03, cash 2.05 first → card 9.98 → 12.03. G 12.03, card 10.00 first → cash 2.05 → 12.05. G 12.02, card 12.00 → card due 0.02. `up` G 12.03, card 12.00 → card 0.03 or cash 0.05. All match #20b.
- **`none` seed data:** step `NULL` = 1 minor unit = no rounding in B9, the column text, the `none` seed, the hub default for a non-PKR tenant and #14c. `PK` = `none` with step 100. No contradiction is left.
- **Adapter settings hold no secrets:** :547 and A-1 (3) both say so. `settingsSchema` must not define secret fields, and #74 fails one that does. Secrets are only in the hub-only `fiscal_credentials`, never synced (B22). `SetFiscalToken` never echoes the token, and `FiscalTokenSet` carries ids, `name` and time only.
- **`base_urls` and credits / pending rows:** `base_url`, `settings` and `credentials` are all read for `fiscal_outbox.adapter_key`. A credit copies its original's adapter (:808, :838). So a pending PRA row or a PRA credit after a jurisdiction switch is sent with PRA's URL, settings and token. `environment` stays per branch, which is acceptable: one branch, one environment.
- **`seed-tenant.ts` ISO 4217 row (H-5):** right file (`seed-tenant.ts:53` is today the only writer of `tenants.currency_code`). The right 00 section (v1.11 §6.26 :1405), cited in F-6 / P0.3 / #14c, and the dependency already exists.
- **H10-8 texts:** all four edits are in. No core "paisa" or "per branch" currency text is left outside history and Pakistan-only examples.
- **Privacy, as before:** buyer tax ID only in `invoice_buyers`. `hashOmit` and `VALIDATION_FAILED` paths unchanged (#43). Credentials never in events, results, hashes, logs or `fiscal-status`. `config` / `settings` never logged. The new `name` column and event field are not secret.
