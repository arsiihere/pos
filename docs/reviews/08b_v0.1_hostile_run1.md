**Verdict: FIX, THEN RUN 2 (full).** The core idea holds. Recipes are cloud config with immutable published versions. The cost engine is pure and works in BigInt fractions. The snapshot carries cost **inputs**, not results. The bump rule is tied to snapshot content. What breaks:

- **Graph safety.** Two concurrent publishes can form a cycle (the spec's own race test asserts the opposite). Depth is checked only downward. Restore skips the graph check. The engine throws on the result, and on the hub that throw lands at bill close.
- **Release and hub delivery.** No release bump. The snapshot body depends on build time. A bad inventory key blocks the whole menu config on the hub.
- **Owner edits.** Edits made elsewhere (sale unit, option linked, owner stock-item kind) silently invalidate a published recipe, and one of them puts a secret BOM into a branch snapshot.
- **Contracts.** The contracts 08c and 08d need are short of what they ask for.

Most MAJORs have a one-line fix. Cutting `ck_only` / packet recipes from the pilot (see "Cuts") removes M-4 (c), M-10 and part of B13 / B16.
