## 5. Verdict

**FIX THEN RE-REVIEW.** 0 blockers, 7 majors, 13 minors (down from 16 / 17).

The majors cluster in three places:
- **Lock vs holder (1, 2, 3):** the activity fold reads another projection during catch-up; `requireLock` ignores who holds the seating; a holder change leaves the old holder's lock blocking the new one.
- **Owner rules (4, 5):** the R23 rule cannot be satisfied by handing over, and slips after a move still mix two parties under one `table_key`.
- **Edges (6, 7):** an empty seating cannot be handed over, and a billed-then-cancelled seating cannot be cleaned.

Finding 4 needs the owner (R23 says "sends a request"). Every other major has a mechanical fix. Fix all seven and the minors in v0.5. **Run 3 is needed**, because fixes 1–3 change §3.3, §4.2 and §5 again.
