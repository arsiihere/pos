## BLOCKER

### R2-B-1 — The re-plan has no "early" step: a held line placed early at submit goes late at the next re-plan (Q-PK-7 broken)
**Where:** §3.5.8 PK-2 chain (l. 862), PK-7 (l. 871: "a line the chain placed earlier than `d(L) − max_early_ms` keeps its booking when pass 1's window has no fit"), §3.5.3 pass 1 pseudo-code (l. 751–754, unchanged), BQ-2 (l. 760), PK-3 (l. 867), tests 178 (d), 185 (l. 1873, 1880).
**Problem:** chain step (2) books a held line before `d_p − max_early_ms` (the second shake, the 2nd–5th drink of a shared `p`). Its deadline stays `d_p` (PK-2, l. 864). At the next re-plan of that station (any later submit, void or capacity event), pass 1 tries `latestFit(max(t, d − max_early), d − keep)`, then `latestFit(lo, d)`, then puts the line in the **late set**. It never tries `latestFit(t, d)`. The §3.5.6 search also bounds it below by `max(t, d − max_early_ms)` (`replan.ts:506`), so the line lands **after `p`** while the slot it held before `p` is free. PK-7's "keeps its booking" clause does not fix this:
- (a) it is not in the pass-1 pseudo-code, `replan.ts` or §11;
- (b) it covers only a line "placed earlier than the window". Two held lines with the same `d` swap: one in-window drink A at `[p−3, p−1)` and one early drink B at `[p−5, p−3)`, both `d = p − 2`. Pass 1 takes B first (the tie-break is weight × waiting, then id) and gives B A's window. A then has no fit and goes to the late set;
- (c) "keep its booking" is not defined once lines placed earlier in pass 1 overlap that booking. Kept that way, the station is over capacity.
Batch: BQ-2 (`replan.ts:552–553`) re-places a load with `lo ≥ d(b) − max_early_ms`, else "late at its earliest fit after the load before it". A held load that chain step (2) formed early meets the same problem.
Test 178 (d) (five orders submitted +0…+4 min, one drink each, same `p`) runs pass 1 over the older drinks at every submit, so it fails, or passes only because of the tie-break order. This breaks the accepted Q-PK-7 rule ("never late while the kitchen can make the pickup") and R4.
**Fix:** for a held line, give pass 1 (slot) and BQ-2 (batch) the same chain as PK-2: `… else latestFit(max(t, …), d)` with lower bound `t`, then the earlier load before it on a batch station, and only then the late set. Also widen the §3.5.6 lower bound for a held line to `t`. Delete the "keeps its booking" clause. Name `replan.ts` and `batch.ts` in §11. Tests: 178 (d) followed by a sixth, unrelated submit and a capacity re-plan on the counter, with no drink landing after `p`; the A/B swap case; a batch station with two early held loads.

## MAJOR

### R2-M-1 — A held group can slip, but its slip resolves at once: resolution still counts sync groups only
**Where:** §3.8 (l. 959, PK-6 bullet); §3.8 slip state (l. 961), AD-21 R3 / R4 (l. 965); PK-6 (l. 870); test 190. Code: `slip.ts:29–33` ("R4 a key's slip resolves when every order … has every sync group resolved"), `slip.ts:196–216` (`syncGroups`, `isResolved = awaiting === 0 && syncGroups(...).every(groupResolved)`).
**Problem:** PK-6 changes only the crossing: `slipCrossing` / `keyDue` admit held groups. Resolution, R3 "only their sync lines count … for resolution" and R5 are not touched. Three results:
- A **drinks-only pickup order** has no sync group, so it counts as resolved at once (an empty "every" is true). The first `fires_alerts` pass that raises the slip then closes it `done` (§3.8 "Closing"), or the slip never sticks.
- In a mixed order, a done burger resolves the key while the held drink is still late: the alert closes `done` on a late drink.
- `slipGroupsAt` (`slip.ts:391–399`) lists sync groups only, so the card's estimate and target miss the held line, although l. 959 says "the card's `lines` include the held line".
**Fix:** say in §3.8 (R3 and R4 readings, slip state, `slipGroupsAt`) that for an order with `pickup_at_ms ≠ null` "the order's slip groups" = its sync groups plus its held immediate groups, for the crossing, the estimate, `lines` and resolution alike. After a boost (PK-5) the set shrinks back to sync groups, and an open slip on a held group then resolves. Tests 190 (d) drinks-only pickup order: slip raised, not closed until the drink is done. 190 (e) mixed order, burger done and drink late: alert stays open.

### R2-M-2 — OWNER: Boost clears the pickup, so whole-order readiness is switched off for the early customer
**Where:** PK-5 (l. 869: "planned as an order without pickup (… readiness, slip)"); To 03b #12 (l. 1240: "After a boost … today's rule applies"); test 191 last clause; 03b v1.0.16 test 74 (l. 658: "the same order after a boost … is Ready with the drink waiting"), 03b §3.4 OR-2 (l. 207: "Handed to customer" **enabled when the card is Ready**); 02b v1.0.25 test 67.
**Problem:** Q-PK-1 accepted "Boost = the customer is here, cook now". That is the moment R4's "drinks wait too" matters most, because the customer is standing at the counter. Once `pickup_at_ms` is `null`, the Pass mirror and `groupReadiness` ignore immediate lines. The bag goes green with the drink unpoured, the counter ready alert (R8) rings, and OR-2's "Handed to customer" is enabled, so the bag can be handed over without the drink. The Q-PK-1 text (§12.3 l. 2139, status file l. 32) speaks only of placement. The readiness change was never put to the owner. The Pass also learns about the boost only at the next catch-up (03b test 110), so client and engine disagree in between.
**Recommended default:** keep `EngineOrder.pickup_at_ms` at the boost and use the existing `EngineOrder.boosted` (`state.ts:118`). The held placement, the held slip and the PK-2 floor apply when `pickup_at_ms ≠ null ∧ ¬boosted`. Whole-order readiness (To 03b #12) applies when `pickup_at_ms ≠ null`, boosted or not. This needs no new field or event, and version 6 is bumped anyway. 02b `open-orders` / 03b `point_view` then show "boosted", not `null`. Rewrite tests 186, 191, 03b 74, 110.

### R2-M-3 — Two placement rules still carry the run-1 two-step chain (late before early)
**Where:** §3.5.3 AD-14 amendment (l. 769: "placed by PK-2 (latest fit by `d_p` within `max_early_ms`, else `earliestFit(max(t, d_p))`)"); §3.3 BQ-3 note (l. 677: "else forms a load at its latest fit by `d_p`"; held line defined as "an immediate line of a pickup order", without FIFO lines); changes table PK-2 (l. 48) is marked as superseded, but l. 769 and l. 677 are not.
**Problem:** a builder reading §3.5.3 or §3.3 builds the M-1 bug back on FIFO and batch stations. The spec contradicts itself (CLAUDE.md: "If code and spec disagree, STOP").
**Fix:** at l. 769 and l. 677, replace the chain with "the PK-2 / PK-3 chain (§3.5.8)", and use PK-1's definition of a held line.
