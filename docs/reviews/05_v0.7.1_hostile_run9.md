## Walk-through: one non-PK jurisdiction, a full day on paper

Jurisdiction `XX` (test fixture): CAD-like, exponent 2, inclusive prices, profile `food` → [GST 500 bp simple, PST 997.5 bp **compound**], `higher_rate`, cash rounding step 5 `nearest` `applies_to: 'cash'`, buyer `{ threshold: null }`, adapter `none`.

- **Data only:** `jurisdictions`, `jurisdiction_rules`, `jurisdiction_profile_taxes`, `jurisdiction_tax_rates` (both classes and both integration statuses alike). That is enough for compute, tax rows and the bill / invoice shape. It is **not** enough for: the receipt registration numbers (H9-5); a Canadian adapter's seller fields if one were needed (H9-4); weighed lines (H9-8); menu prices if the branch shares a PKR tenant (H9-1); client formatting (H9-7).
- **Bill:** 2 × 12.00 + 8.50 inclusive, SC 10 % on the pre-tax net. Ran (2) gives TI 436 spread 141 / 295, N 2 814, SC 281 taxed 14 + 29, G 35.74 = menu 32.50 + SC 2.81 + SC taxes 0.43. Σ tax rows = 4.79 = printed. Cash rounds to 35.75.
- **Splits:** equal-3 gives 3 invoices, each with its own back-calculation (±1 tax per split vs the whole bill, by design). The item split and the discount frozen at the first close work as for Pakistan. A void on the open part re-caps the discount (B7).
- **Payments:** all card 35.74 closes exact. Card 20.00 + cash hits H9-2. Tendered 40.00 cash gives change 4.25 on the receiving shift. Reopen with no change gives credit 35.75 + new 35.75 (H9-9 if the mode changed in between).
- **Z:** one currency (one branch). Net = sales − credits; tax by (code, profile, class). The invariant holds with brought / carried forward (#66).
- **0 and 3 minor digits:** exponent 0, step 1, gives no rounding and a correct percent discount floor. Exponent 3 on `none` rounds to 1 000 fils (H9-3); with an explicit step 5 it is fine.

## What holds (checked, not findings)

- **§3.5 in both price modes:** the inclusive multiplier for compound lists is correct, including a simple tax after a compound one (a_k sums every earlier a_j). Exclusive compound taxes use the rounded earlier taxes on the same part. `G = N + SC + Σ rows` holds (Ran (2)). One tax reduces to v0.6.2.
- **`pro_rata` over tax lists:** the binary search is monotone in `x` for any list of non-negative rates, so the declared-amount principle survives J-2.
- **Privacy, every buyer ID type:** `invoice_buyers (id_type, id_value)`, `hashOmit` on all of `buyer`, `VALIDATION_FAILED` paths without values, last 4 in the print queue and on credit notes, full number only on that receipt. No v0.7 path leaks a value.
- **Port core:** idempotency = `invoice_ref`; at-least-once delivery; answer outcomes; `ordering` per `pos_id`; a credit behind its original; `credit` capability + `credit_support`; catch-up window as data (it drives the stuck alert only, which is enough for the pilot). Appendix P still gives the PRA build everything v0.6.2 had, with A1–A13 kept open and the STOP at task 30 intact.
