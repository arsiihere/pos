### B-2 — A held line booked before the sync loop takes the pickup slot; the order's own sync lines then land late on an idle station
**Where:** §3.5.1 step 3–4 (l. 673–674), PK-2 (l. 816–817, "against the step-3 profile … Its interval is added to the profile as in step 3"), PK-5 (l. 824, "immediate lines … re-placed by PK-2 … **before** the one hold-and-fire over its sync lines"), test 31 extended (l. 1604), test 178 last sentence (l. 1805), test 186 (l. 1813). Code: `placeSequence` places step 3 then step 4 against the updated profile (`plan.ts:412–437`); `trySyncAt` serialises against **sync siblings only** (`chainStart`, `plan.ts:198`, `:213–224`; `base` = the profile *after* step 3, `:196`); `boostOrder` places immediates first, then `holdAndFire` (`plan.ts:624–635`).
**Problem:** today an immediate line goes at `t`, so it never competes with its own order's sync lines at `T`. Under PK-2 it is booked first at `[p − carry − cook_I, p − carry)`, exactly where the order's sync line on the same station must end. Worked case, one slot station capacity 1, empty, `p = +60 min`, carry 0: sync burger (cook 10) + immediate fries (cook 5). Step 3 books fries at `[55, 60)`. Step 4: `f0 = fireEndingAt(60)` → `[50, 60)` overlaps the fries; `cs` is undefined (fries are not a sync sibling), so `a = earliestFit(base, 50) − 50 = 60 − 50` and `T` advances to 70: **the burger lands 10 min after the pickup, the promise reads p + 10 + padding, with the station idle for 55 minutes.** The same happens at every boost (PK-5). The spec's tests do not see it because the "mixed order" of test 178 and test 186 is the disturbance kit on `SLOT(2)` (`disturbances.test.ts:1137`). Test 31 extended is internally impossible: the kit is `S1` capacity 1 (`plan.test.ts:175`), the sync line's interval ends at `pickup − 30 000` (`:189`), and the extension requires the immediate line's interval to end at `pickup − carry` as well. R4 is "the **whole** order lands for the pickup time"; this draft makes the whole order late for the most ordinary takeaway (a counter that both fries and holds a drink, a tandoor with naan sync and a ready item).
**Fix:** place a pickup order's held lines **after** its sync groups (step 4 first, then the held lines by PK-2 against the profile that includes the group's bookings), or treat them as D36 siblings: in `trySyncAt` a held line of the same order on the same station joins `chainStart` and ends where the sync siblings start. Either way say what a held line does when the only free time is before its sync siblings (this ties to M-1: with `max_early_ms` 60 000 the fries could not move to `[45, 50)` and would go late instead). State it for `boostOrder` too. Tests: capacity 1, sync 10 + immediate 5 on the same station, `p = +60` → group `T = p`, both lines land by `p`; the same at a boost; test 31's extension on a second station or with the new rule stated.

### B-3 — "The customer came early" has no path: Q-PK-1 closes the boost, and the "02b pickup edit" it points to has no engine contract
**Where:** PK-5 (l. 824), Q-PK-1 (l. 2065), To 02 #15 (g) (l. 1171), PK-1 "the engine reads the held `EngineOrder.pickup_at_ms`; `order_header.pickup_at_ms` only seeds an order the engine does not hold" (l. 815), PK-11 "No new field, event, endpoint" (l. 830). Code: `pickup_at_ms` is written only when an `EngineOrder` is created (`apply-event.ts:335–360`, `newOrder`); `planOrder` reads `proposed.order.pickup_at_ms` (`plan.ts:1324`) while the fold reads `order.pickup_at_ms` (`plan.ts:962`).
**Problem:** a takeaway customer who arrives at 1:30 for a 2:00 order is the most common deviation of a pickup order. With Q-PK-1's proposed answer the boost lands nothing before `p`, so the boost is useless; "Send what's ready" exists only as the answer to a slip of a sync group (§3.8), and a held line never slips (M-4). The draft's answer is "a 02b pickup edit" (To 02 #15 (g)), but nothing can move `EngineOrder.pickup_at_ms` after the order is placed: there is no event, the fold of `OrderLinesAdded.order_header` does not overwrite a held order, and PK-11 says no event is added. If 02b nevertheless changed its own pickup and sent the new value in a later `ProposedOrder`, `planOrder` (proposed header) and the fold (held value) would plan the addition differently and the stored promise would not match the fold (P-2, §3.5.1 "one sequence"). So the approved answer of Q-PK-1 decides whether v1.2.11 needs a new event, a new D23 trigger and a fixture: the spec cannot be approved with it open.
**Fix:** the owner answers Q-PK-1 before approval. Either (a) a boost of a pickup order drops the floor for the whole order (B3 revised for pickup orders, stated as an owner ruling; PK-5 becomes "PK-9 for every line", one rule, no new event) — this reviewer's recommendation, because "boost" already means "this customer now" in the floor UI; or (b) add a `PickupTimeSet { order_id, pickup_at_ms }` event (02b writer, fixture, fold = a re-plan of the order's unfired lines toward the new `p` with the D23 rules, promise of every group recomputed or explicitly kept) and drop "no new event" from PK-11. In both cases state that `planOrder` reads `state.orders[order_id]?.pickup_at_ms` for an order the engine holds (else the proposed header), so plan = fold for additions, and add that test.

## MAJOR

### M-1 — "Late before early": at the default `max_early_ms` two held lines for one pickup on a one-unit counter make the order late
**Where:** PK-2 (l. 817: "when no fire in that window fits, `fire = earliestFit(max(t, d_p))`"), the "Why `latestFit`" note (l. 821), test 178 (l. 1805: "with `max_early_ms` 1 min it lands after the first, target = its land (`> p`)").
**Problem:** `max_early_ms` defaults to 60 000 (01a A-11). The note's own motivation ("the second drink of one order on a one-unit counter would land after the pickup although it fits a minute earlier") holds only when the second drink's cook ≤ `max_early_ms`. For two 2-minute shakes, the second goes **after** the pickup, and test 178 enshrines exactly that. Pickups cluster on round times (2:00, 2:30): five takeaway orders for 2:00 with one drink each on a one-unit drinks counter give drinks at 2:00, 2:01, 2:02 … although the counter was idle all afternoon. For a dine-in sync line "early" means a cold plate at the table; for a takeaway pickup the customer is standing at the counter, so being late is the worse failure. The draft picks late silently, without an owner question, and unlike D36's sync chain (which packs same-order siblings back from `T` ignoring `max_early_ms`, F l. 913).
**Fix:** a three-step chain for a held line: `latestFit(max(t, d_p − max_early_ms), d_p)`, else **`latestFit(t, d_p)`** (ready early, beyond the knob, never late while the kitchen can make it), else `earliestFit(max(t, d_p))`. Or ask the owner (Q-PK-6: "early beyond the item's ready-early limit, or late?") with that as the proposal. Rewrite test 178's second case accordingly and add "five orders, one drink each, same `p`, one-unit counter".

### M-2 — A pickup order's sync line routed to a FIFO alternate inside the hold loop still fires at `t`
**Where:** PK-4 (l. 823: covers "an immediate line, or a sync line placed in step 3 by AD-14"), AD-14 amendment (l. 725: "a mixed-option line may land on a FIFO station at its earliest fit (never held), placed after the batch's all-FIFO lines in `syncOrder`"), §11 file list (l. 1917, no `trySyncAt`, `holdAndFire`, `replanEnded`, `requeueGroup`). Code: `trySyncAt` FIFO branch `plan.ts:204–210` (`earliestFit(here, t, …)`, fits when `land ≤ T`).
**Problem:** a sync line whose allowed stations are mixed (home Waada Guard, a FIFO alternate) is not a step-3 line; when its home is busy at `T − carry` the loop takes the FIFO alternate at `earliestFit(t)`, which for a 2:00 pickup lands it at 1:05. PK-4 does not touch this path, the task list does not name it, and tests 184 / 189 have no mixed-option line. The same branch runs in every re-loop (`holdAndFire` from void / remove / requeue). That is a held line of a pickup order going out at its earliest fit, the exact case R4 forbids.
**Fix:** in `trySyncAt`, a FIFO option of a pickup order's line is placed by PK-2 toward `T − carry` (latest fit ending by `T − carry` within `max_early_ms`, else the FIFO earliest fit ≥ that), with the FIFO land still flooring `T`. Name `trySyncAt` in §11. Test 184 gains "home busy at `p`, FIFO alternate free → the line lands at `p` on the alternate, not at its earliest fit".

### M-3 — The Pass reads a pickup order as Ready while its held lines are unmade; "no To 03b change" is wrong
**Where:** changes table l. 61 ("no To 03b change: the KDS sees a held line when it fires, as any line"), PK-2 bullet "the line keeps `serve_mode: 'immediate'` … 03b readiness … treat it as an immediate line" (l. 820), To 02 #15 (b) ("… is 02b's and 03b's to keep for the hand-over", l. 1171), §6.1i (l. 1391). Code: `groupReadiness` counts **sync lines only** ("Immediate items are never held"; "Order READY = no group has anything waiting", `view.ts:152–154`). 03b v1.0.16 §3.4 l. 153 ("Ready … when no sync line here is `added` / `cooking`"), test 74 l. 603 ("immediate line green").
**Problem:** for a pickup order the held drink is `added` until about `p`. The engine's readiness and the Pass client's mirror of it ignore it, so an order whose sync lines are done shows **Ready** with the drinks unpoured, and a drinks-only pickup order shows Ready at submit. `MarkReadyToServe` then raises the takeaway ready alert to the placer and every kiosk (03b v1.0.16 R8, §6.21) for an order that is not ready, and "Pack next" (03b l. 159) sorts on it. R4's "drinks wait too" is a promise about the hand-over, which is exactly what 03b computes. The station-less and zone lines 02b / 03b must "keep for the hand-over" have no To 03b row either.
**Fix:** add To 03b #11 (and a §6.1i change): for a pickup order, a held line counts as **waiting** in `groupReadiness` and in the Pass client's rule until it is done; a held line is never HOLD-hinted. Name the 03b bump in an R26 row. Tests: drinks-only pickup order → not Ready until the drink is done; mixed order → Ready only when both are.

### M-4 — A held line has a customer promise but never slips (Q-PK-4)
**Where:** PK-6 (l. 825), §3.8 note (l. 914), Q-PK-4 (l. 2068), test 181 (l. 1808: "no slip is raised for it").
**Problem:** AD-21 R3 ("only sync groups slip") was a reading for lines that go at their earliest fit: their promise is "as soon as possible", so lateness against it is noise. A held line has a real future promise (`promise_ms = p + padding`, printed on the R6 token slip and shown to the customer). It can be made late after submit by a priority group (PR-5 pushes waiting lines later), a capacity drop, an overrun cascade, a day-close release back to full cook (PK-10), or a recount (PK-8) — and nobody hears. The draft's fallback, "the not-collected alert is 03b's (R8)", fires after the order is *ready*, i.e. for the opposite problem. Max delay (PR-3) measures from `T_L` too, so it fires only past `T_L + limit` (default up to 30 min).
**Fix:** recommend the alternative as the answer: **held immediate groups of a pickup order slip** like sync groups (the slip filter in `slipCrossing` / `keyDue` admits a group whose order has `pickup_at_ms ≠ null`; same key `takeaway:<order_id>`, same card, "Send what's ready" sends its done lines). Add a test: a held drink pushed past `p + slip_threshold_ms` by a capacity drop → `SlipWarned`.

### M-5 — Stock is reserved at submit for a pickup up to a business day away; R4 is silent and the draft decides it without an owner question
**Where:** PK-8 (l. 827: "Reserving at the fire instead is not proposed (R4 is silent)"), §3.7 note (l. 873), test 179 (l. 1806).
**Problem:** with "latest pickup = end of the business day", a 10:00 order for 21:00 takes the last three pre-cooked karahi and holds them until its Start at about 20:40. Every walk-in in between gets the full cook, or `sold_out` is **refused** at available 0 (§3.7 l. 871). A recount releases the **newest** reservation first (§3.7 table), so the walk-in loses again. v1.2.10 never met this because no hub has ever written a pickup (l. 41); v1.2.11 is the change that makes it real, and it extends it to ready-stock lines (cold drinks, packed items) that previously were reserved and consumed within seconds. CLAUDE.md lists stock reservation as one of the P1 races this tier exists for. "R4 is silent" is the definition of an owner question, not a reason to skip one.
**Fix:** add Q-PK-6 with both options and their cost: (a) reserve at submit (as drafted: first submit wins, stock held for hours); (b) reserve at the freeze (or fire) of the held line, with the submit showing "from stock if still there" and a fallback to the full cook stated (needs a reservation writer on the tick, a statement count and a `StockReserved` producer row). Keep (a) as the proposal if the owner prefers it, but get it ruled. Test: walk-in after a pickup order that holds the last unit → full cook (or refused), as ruled.

### M-6 — "No perf impact" is asserted, not measured; held lines turn a fired-at-once set into an hours-long hidden set
**Where:** PK-12 (l. 831), §7.1 note (l. 1501), changes table PK-12 "— (`test:perf` unchanged)" (l. 58). Code: `pivotOn` prunes only older lines whose interval ends ≤ the pivot (`replan.ts:251–294`); `test/sim/generate.ts:157–162` sets every sim pickup to `at + max cook + 300 s`; §3.5.6 re-places **all** hidden lines of a station.
**Problem:** the cost per line is unchanged, but the number of lines is not. Today an immediate line is fired within seconds and becomes fixed; under v1.2.11 every immediate, ready and from-stock line of every pickup order stays **hidden** until about `p`, so it is in `free_old` of every later submit on its station (it ends after any pivot, so D25 never prunes it) and in every overload search there. A drinks counter with 20 open pickup orders adds 20–40 hidden lines to each submit's pass 1 on it. The simulator cannot see this: it has sync lines only and pickups 5 minutes out, so "its numbers do not move" proves nothing about the new case. The P-11 bound (270 re-placed lines) and the 50 ms p99 are gates (§7.1 l. 1488: "If task 18 misses the gate, the build stops and the spec is bumped first").
**Fix:** add a §10.6 day with pickup orders: e.g. 30 % of takeaways with `pickup_at_ms` 30–240 min ahead and ≥ 1 immediate / ready line each, on a one-unit drinks counter; measure re-placed lines and p99 on the owner's machine (`pnpm test:perf`, CLAUDE.md) before approval, or write the stop rule into the plan task ("if the pickup day exceeds 270 / 50 ms, stop and bump"). State the expected hidden-set size in PK-12.

### M-7 — D62: the fold's meaning changes, so `ENGINE_STATE_VERSION` must bump (or the foundation must grant an exception)
**Where:** PK-11 (l. 830), changes table (l. 56, 61), §11 (l. 1917), test 189 (l. 1816). Foundation v1.17 l. 1332 ("bumped whenever the `EngineState` shape **or the meaning of `applyEvent`** changes; a CI check fails if either changes without a bump"), §6.7 fixture rule l. 1689; 03a test 56 (l. 1629).
**Problem:** v1.2.11 changes what `applyEvent(ItemAdded)` at `awaiting = 0` and `applyEvent(OrderBoosted)` produce for a pickup order. The draft argues "no log holds one yet" and makes correctness depend on an **install order** ("installed before 02b v1.0.25 writes a non-null pickup", To 02 #15 (f)) that nothing enforces. The opposite direction is unguarded too: roll the hub back to a v1.2.10 build after pickup orders exist and it reads the v5 snapshot (same version) and folds the rest of the day with the old meaning — exactly what D62's snapshot fixture exists to catch. If test 189 adds pickup orders with held lines to the fixture log, the v5 fixture changes and the D62 CI check fails by its own rule.
**Fix:** bump `ENGINE_STATE_VERSION` to 6 (no shape change; cost: one replay in the maintenance hour, §3.12, already budgeted) and regenerate the fixture with pickup orders in its log. If the owner prefers no bump, put a D62 exception in the B-1 foundation bump ("meaning changes reachable only by inputs no shipped writer has produced") and add a guard: 02b v1.0.25 refuses a non-null `pickup_at_ms` unless `@pos/engine` exports the capability (`PICKUP_HOLDS_WHOLE_ORDER = true`).
