### R3-M-1 — Board exclusion leaves independent delivery and receipt paths unspecified

**Location** §§3.4.3, 3.7 ll.649–650, 849; §6 batch 3 ll.1819, 1824–1835; §10.9i tests 116–120.

**Evidence and impact** The guard enumerates `alert`, `alert_seen`, `alert_closed`, catch-up and receipt credit, while `binding_route` is a separate after-commit sender using `sendToDevices` (`apps/hub/src/alerts/alert-sockets.ts:236–255`, `apps/hub/src/ws/server.ts:137–140`). Receipt admission is also separate. It accepts from cumulative device or inbox rows or the prior sent marker (`apps/hub/src/alerts/receipts.ts:204–234`). Test 117 checks a later close against a socket already closed and therefore does not exercise a selected destination during conversion. 05a §6.4 ll.542–547 ends staff/admin sessions as part of the conversion, and 00 HPA §6.21 ll.1732–1733 closes alert mode with 4413. Alert hooks run before generic publish (`apps/hub/src/engine-host/writer.ts:83–90`), so socket teardown alone is insufficient as the alert guard.

**Fix** Require the current-profile predicate at recipient/route planning, each alert sender including `binding_route`, both reconnect builders before `recordSent`, and receipt intake before its SELECT. Keep pre-conversion receipt history, including receipts already accepted into the buffer. Make hook order explicit: refresh the profile map before alert fan-out, and remove the converted device from the alert-mode registry when closing it with 4413. Test a conversion with a cumulative recipient and an already-selected destination, including `alert`, `alert_seen`, `alert_closed`, `binding_route` and a late receipt. Assert zero frames/credit, zero catch-up alert rows, and preserved keys with no alert-mode retry.

### R3-M-2 — Removing a counter screen leaves its card visible on the surviving session

**Location** §3.5 l.748; §6 batch 3 ll.1818–1822; §8.1 ll.1862–1865; §10.9i test 109 l.2113.

**Evidence and impact** The derived rule says the device keeps its row and “gets `lock`” after re-profiling. Test 109 checks only catch-up. A screen-only edit can keep the staff session (`apps/hub/src/commands/set-device-profile.ts:137–146`). The client handles `device_profile` by updating screens and access (`apps/client/src/features/staff/StaffApp.tsx:344–350`), while the alert store keeps the card until a replacement view arrives (`apps/client/src/features/alerts/store.ts:131–146,258–267,281–300`). A counter-only kiosk that loses the screen and is then omitted from subsequent steps can show its old card for the rest of the alert.

**Fix** On a screen-key change, refresh the signed-in socket's complete `alerts` catch-up through the existing builder once for that device/session. Apply the refreshed view before continuing to render stale counter cards. Preserve the alert/step notification key so the refresh is silent for a shown step. Extend test 109 with a connected recipient that loses the screen after its last targeted step. Assert the open row switches to lock without a reconnect or a new ring.

### R3-M-4 — A second counter sees the same alert ID without requester rights or a pending-state contract

Test two eligible cashiers on separate devices, dedupe, a rejection and a subsequent fresh ask. Keep the 01d requester and withdraw rules intact.
