## 3. Attack results by area (hand-built)

### 3.1 Sliding interval of a fired-not-started line

- **Capacity 1, one fired line (test 96).** A is fired at 12:00 and not started. At 12:06 its live interval is `[12:06, 12:11)`, so B lands on `[12:11, 12:16)` (`T` 12:16, `P` 12:19). A is started at 12:08 (`[12:08, 12:13)`). The cascade's smallest fitting delay for B is 2 min (one step), giving `[12:13, 12:18)`, which is ≤ `P`. **Correct.**
- **Capacity 2, one fired line.** At 12:06, A slides to `[12:06, 12:11)` on one unit, and N takes the second unit at `[12:06, 12:11)`. A's Start at 12:08 needs no shift. **Correct.**
- **Capacity 1, two fired lines.** Both slide to the same window. N's promise breaks by 3 min after one Start that was 7 min late (#1). **Wrong.**
- **Capacity 2, four fired lines.** All four lie on `[t, t + 5)`, while really two of them run `[t + 5, t + 10)`. A new line placed at `t + 5` is pushed 5 min (#1). **Wrong.**
- **A late Start with a queue.** The tick fires the planned successor at its `fire_ms` regardless of whether the station is free (S:374). One late Start is therefore enough to produce the two-fired-line case. That is the everyday trigger.
- **COOKING overrun + one fired line.** The COOKING end is `max(planned, t)`. Its unknown future extension is the F §4.7 D33 rule, which the cascade and the slip cover. That is accepted, not a finding.

### 3.2 Abandon exit

- **Against a slow table.** A table whose lines are all served exits by the 30-min rule, and its additions become new orders (To 02 #1). An order with a done-but-unserved line leaves `order_abandon_ms` after its last fold. That is intended, but the To 02 label blames the kitchen (#15). No false exit was found for a table that is still being served, because any Served tap refreshes the clock.
- **Against an unfired line.** An unfired line is fired at `fire_ms ≤ d ≤ T` (bounded, also under §3.5.6 with the `max_late_ms` cap), and its `FireDispatched` refreshes the clock. A delivery held 6 h ahead is never abandoned (test 111). **Correct.**
- **Against a long cook.** A legitimate COOKING line can be abandoned mid-cook (#10).
- **Slip state.** It never resolves (#8).
- **Removal.** A late tap on an exited but still present order first runs the removal step (the order is gone), then the absent-id no-op. No resurrection. This is consistent with S:271, though implied rather than stated for folds other than `OrderLinesAdded`.

### 3.3 Planning at the last listed `ItemAdded` with `StockReserved` after it

- **Batch.** `OrderPlaced` (with `lines[].from_stock`), then `ItemAdded` × n, then `StockReserved` × k. The fold plans at the n-th `ItemAdded`, with each line's cook time taken from `OrderPlaced.lines[].from_stock`. The `StockReserved` folds that follow flip nothing, so they do not re-plan. **Correct**, live and in replay.
- **Split appends.** Split over two `tx.append` calls, the plan runs at the first append's `recorded_at` in both live and replay (the counts are in the first event). **Correct.**
- **Multi-draw lines.** These get k > n `StockReserved` events, none of which re-plan. **Correct.**
- **Gap.** `planOrder`'s inputs cannot decide `from_stock` (#6).

### 3.4 `StockReserved` from_stock-flip re-plan after `StockAdded`

- **Slot, hidden line** (fresh 20 min, stock 2 min). The flip sets `cook_ms` = 2 and the deadline becomes `T − 2`. Pass 1 re-places the line later within its slack, and `T` and `P` are unchanged. **Correct.**
- **Slot, frozen line.** It is a candidate, and in deadline order it is taken first, which is wrong (#4).
- **Batch, line in a hidden load.** It switches to its stock program, the P load re-forms without it, and it joins a Q load. **Correct**, but the line cannot later return to P (#5).
- **Batch, line in a frozen or fired load.** It is not a candidate. **Correct.**
- **Replay equality.** The fold reads only the state and the event, and candidates are chosen by the handler and recorded as events, so replay = live. Two drafts in one command miss a multi-draw line (#12). The cost is ungated (#11).

### 3.5 Release rules per cause

- **Recount with a multi-draw line.** The line is released whole, on X and Y. **Correct.**
- **Frozen batch load.** It is untouched by the recount and the close. **Correct.**
- **Close.** It releases hidden lines plus exited lines only. **Correct.**
- **Abandon.** It releases fired-not-started lines from the tick; COOKING lines have already consumed. **Correct.**
- **Wrong cases.** A partial void, and Q14 on one item of a multi-draw line (#2). The allowed `on_hand < reserved` state underflows at Start and halts a `single_tap` station's tick (#3).

### 3.6 Overrun `nextDueAt`

With a queue behind the overrunning line, each boundary writes a `ReservationsShifted` and moves `nextDueAt`. **Correct.** With an empty queue, the tick is due every pass (#9).

### 3.7 The pure checkpoint predicate

`noOrderInEngine` checks the predicate over `state.orders` plus live reservations of exited lines, in O(orders), only while the deferred checkpoint is pending. Test 112's 23:20 / 23:50 case passes. The `last_seq` and body read in one `setImmediate` are consistent (`writer.ts:86`: every commit is whole). The one divergence is an abandoned order's `slips` entry (#8). Leaked reservations from #2 would be carried in `stock`, which is consistent with live but wrong in itself.

### 3.8 Slip threshold vs padding

With the defaults (threshold 2 min, padding 3 min), on Waada Guard stations the slip is raised 1 min before the promise is blown. **Correct.** On EDF / FIFO stations (padding 0) it is raised after the promise (#14). With carry > 0 it is raised `carry` late (#7).
