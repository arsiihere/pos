### R2-M-1: §8 still says "an unsent cart shows its total on screen only", which contradicts OR-5
**Where:** §8 l. 550 ("**Print check bill** = 05 `PrintCheckBill` … on every token with an unpaid bill and on the cart of a **sent** token; an unsent cart shows its total on screen only (owner decision D-7)").
**Problem:** Under OR-5, which is binding, an unsent cart prints through 05 `PrintCartCheckBill`, and a paid token offers "Print receipt again". §4.3 l. 364 and §12.3 l. 712 already say this, but §8 is the client contract, and the 02b-TK client plan will be built from it.
**Evidence:** 03c l. 364 vs l. 550; 02b v1.0.25 l. 842 (already updated); 05 §8 l. 1531 (already updated).
**Fix:** Strike the clause and replace it, marked "⚠️ Changed by v1.0.16 owner review (OR-5)": "an **unsent** cart → 05 `PrintCartCheckBill { draft }` (05 §6.3a); a sent token with money due → `PrintCheckBill`; once paid → 'Print receipt again' = 05 `PrintBillDocument { receipt }` (`take-payment`)". Add the three cases to §8's client test list.
