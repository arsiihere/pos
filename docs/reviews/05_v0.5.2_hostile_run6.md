## What holds (checked, not findings)

- **Money model:** with the H1-2 / H1-3 cash rule (amount ≤ balance, change on the receiving payment's shift, expected adds amounts only), Σ payments − change = split total, and expected cash = §3.7. The Z invariant "takings = Z net + CF − BF", with BF and CF over **open splits** (H3-3), balances in every case I modelled: reopen and re-close at lower or higher totals; a PARTLY_PAID carry (500 closed, 200 open), reopened on day 2 then closed or left open. A credit equals its original's net payments, so payments that reopen as "open" offset it exactly.
- **§6.31 arithmetic** re-run (Ran 1): 6 624.00 (+0.40), 5 996.00 (+0.50); `pro_rata` 285 714 / 14 286 / 285 286 / 45 646 → 6 309.00 (−0.32).
- **Consumed contracts:** `pendingVoids(db, order_ids, state?)` with `refund_minor` per request (`voids/contracts.ts:66`); single-slot guards held by 04 (`close-business-day.ts:20`, `merge-guard.ts:9`, `line-guard.ts:21`), with rows A-3 / T-1 / Q-3 as stated; `OrdersBilled` folded by 02b (`orders/projection.ts:119-123`); `BillClosed` `.strict()` `{ seating_id }`; `ctx.queuedAt` (`registry.ts:31`); `payloadHash(payload, omit)` with one field (`lib/hash.ts`), so H-4 is needed; `printDocument(tx, …)` and `{ drawer: true }`; stream enum without `bill` (F-1 needed); `registerAuditAction` throws on mapped or sealed types, and `REQUEST_ACTIONS` is a fixed record (V-3 needed); the day-close tick expires non-request alerts (`projections/alerts.ts:345-347`), as `fiscal_outbox_stuck` assumes; a fully voided order becomes `OrderCancelled` (walk-out Q12 ends cleanly); 02b `comp` `apply` re-checks (the precedent for H6-2).
- **Privacy:** zod 4.6.7 issues reach responses as path + message only; no 05 event or `values` carries text after H3-5 / H4-4; the token never reaches a hash (H-4) or an event.
- **Hygiene:** no secret, machine path or CNIC in the spec or the 05 stories; the PRA URLs are public endpoints.
- **YAGNI:** nothing beyond the pilot found other than H6-15's check. `bill_print_jobs`, `shift_takings`, per-device POS ID mapping and `HandoverCount` each trace to a ruling.
