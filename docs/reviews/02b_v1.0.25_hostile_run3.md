### R3-M-8 — Approval survives a later, distinct due obligation — OWNER

**Location** §3.3 L268, §3.7a L321, §6.16 L764, §6.19 L819/L835.

**Problem and evidence** A historical approval timestamp permanently makes the row eligible. `OrdersUnbilled` clears billing and day-close timestamps, leaving approval untouched. A manager can approve a due order, the customer can pay without collecting, and billing can later reopen the order with a larger due. The old approval still unlocks that later obligation. The owner's answers specify who and which orders an approval covers, but give no lifetime across billing changes.

**Fix and recommended default** Scope approval to the current unpaid obligation. Clear the effective approval on bill close/reopen and invalidate it when the due rises above the approved amount before hand-over. Preserve the original event for replay/audit. Specify the projection and query behavior in §6.16 and §6.19 and test approval → paid but uncollected → reopen with a positive due. Owner decision needed on whether an earlier approval may authorize a later or increased balance.
